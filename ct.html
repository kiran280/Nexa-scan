SAFETY & PREPARATION

Your Safety Comes First

CT uses X-rays. Your imaging team will determine whether the examination is appropriate and explain any preparation or precautions that apply to you.

  • ✓ Tell the team if you may be pregnant.
  • ✓ Share relevant medical history and current medications.
  • ✓ Mention allergies or previous reactions to contrast material.
  • ✓ Follow any fasting or other instructions given for your scan.

CT uses ionizing radiation. Contrast material is needed for some, but not all, examinations. Follow the specific advice provided by your healthcare team.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About CT Scans?

Find answers to common questions before your appointment.

Book Your Scan ↗
What is a CT scan?

A CT scan uses X-rays and computer processing to produce cross-sectional images of structures inside the body.

Does a CT scan use radiation?

Yes. CT uses ionizing radiation. Your healthcare team considers the clinical need and appropriate scan protocol.

Do I need to fast before a CT scan?

Not every CT scan requires fasting. Requirements depend on the examination and whether contrast material is planned. Follow your appointment instructions.

Will I need contrast material?

Some CT examinations use contrast material to help show certain structures. Your imaging team will explain whether it is needed and check relevant safety information.

When will I receive my report?

Reporting times vary by examination and clinical workflow. Contact your imaging centre for the expected timeframe.
