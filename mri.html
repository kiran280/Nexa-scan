SAFETY & PREPARATION

Your Safety Comes First

MRI uses a strong magnet, so safety screening is an essential part of the examination.

  • ✓ Tell the team about implants, pacemakers, or metal fragments.
  • ✓ Share relevant medical history and information about possible pregnancy.
  • ✓ Follow instructions about clothing, jewellery, and personal belongings.
  • ✓ Ask the team if you feel anxious in enclosed spaces or need assistance.

Always follow the specific instructions provided by your imaging team. Not every implant or device is compatible with MRI.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About MRI?

Here are answers to common questions before your appointment.

Book Your Scan ↗
Does an MRI use radiation?

MRI does not use ionizing radiation. It uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves to create images.

How should I prepare for an MRI?

Preparation varies by examination. Follow your appointment instructions and tell the team about implants, medical devices, and relevant health information.

Will the MRI scan be painful?

The imaging itself is generally painless, but you may need to remain still in one position. Let the team know if you are uncomfortable.

When will I receive my results?

Reporting times depend on the examination and clinical workflow. Your imaging centre can explain how and when results will be shared.

Can I have an MRI if I have an implant?

Some implants are MRI-compatible under specific conditions and others may not be. Tell the team the exact device details before your scan so safety checks can be completed.
