Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about appointments, scan preparation, reports, and contacting our team.

Still have questions?

Our team can help with appointment enquiries, scan availability, and general preparation questions.

Contact Our Team →

You can submit the appointment enquiry form on this page or contact our centre directly. Our team can guide you through availability and the next steps for scheduling.

Referral requirements depend on the scan type and local requirements. Please contact our team before booking to confirm what documents or referrals you should bring.

Preparation varies for MRI, CT, and ultrasound examinations. Follow the instructions provided by your healthcare team and confirm any fasting, medication, or clothing requirements with the imaging centre beforehand.

Report turnaround times can vary depending on the examination and clinical review. Ask the centre about the expected timeframe when confirming your appointment.

Bring your referral if required, relevant previous scan reports or images, identification, and any appointment confirmation. Follow any additional instructions given by the centre.

Contact the diagnostic centre using your appointment details. The team can explain available alternatives and any rescheduling requirements.
