How should I prepare for my scan?

Preparation varies by examination. Some scans may require fasting, a full bladder or contrast-related screening. Follow the specific instructions provided by the imaging center.

How long does an MRI scan take?

Scan duration depends on the body area and protocol. Allow additional time for safety screening, preparation and any required post-examination instructions.

Is a CT scan different from an MRI?

Yes. CT uses X-rays to produce cross-sectional images, while MRI uses magnetic fields and radio waves. Each has different applications and safety considerations.

Is ultrasound safe during pregnancy?

Ultrasound uses sound waves rather than ionizing radiation and is commonly used during pregnancy when clinically indicated. Your healthcare professional can advise on the appropriate examination.

When will I receive my scan report?

Reporting times depend on the examination and clinical circumstances. Contact the center to confirm the expected reporting time for your appointment.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Referral requirements vary by examination and local rules. Contact Nexa Scan before booking to confirm the documentation required for your scan.
