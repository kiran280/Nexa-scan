HELP US PREPARE FOR YOUR CARE

Information worth sharing.

Tell your clinician or imaging team about relevant health information before your examination.

Implants, medical devices

For MRI in particular, tell staff about pacemakers, implants, metal fragments or any implanted or wearable device. Staff must assess MRI safety before you enter.

Allergies and contrast

Share previous reactions to contrast material and any relevant kidney disease, allergies or medical conditions before a contrast-enhanced examination.

Pregnancy or possibility

Inform your healthcare team if you are pregnant or could be pregnant. They can assess the most appropriate examination and any necessary precautions.

Medication, health history

Share your medication list and relevant medical history. Do not stop prescribed medication unless your clinician specifically tells you to.

Every examination is different.

Never assume you need to fast, drink extra water, stop medication or change your routine. Confirm what applies to your exact scan with your healthcare team.

QUICK ANSWERS

Questions before
your scan?

Here are general answers to common preparation questions. Your own scan instructions may differ.

Need a specific answer?Contact our team
Do I need to fast before my scan?

Not always. Fasting requirements depend on the type of scan and the examination protocol. Follow the instructions provided for your appointment or contact the imaging center to confirm.

Can I take my regular medication?

Do not stop or change prescribed medication on your own. Ask your clinician or imaging team whether any changes are needed for your specific examination.

What should I bring with me?

Bring your appointment details, referral or prescription if requested, relevant previous imaging reports, and information about medications, allergies and medical devices.

Can I have a scan if I might be pregnant?

Tell your clinician and imaging team before the scan. They will assess your circumstances and decide on the appropriate examination and precautions.

How long will my appointment take?

Timing varies with the scan type, body area, preparation and whether additional images are needed. Ask the imaging center for an estimate for your appointment.

When will I receive my results?

Results depend on the examination and reporting process. Your imaging center can explain how and when your report will be made available to you or your referring clinician.
