SAFETY & PREPARATION

Comfort Starts with Preparation

Preparation varies depending on the type of ultrasound and the body area being examined.

  • ✓ Check whether fasting is required for your examination.
  • ✓ Follow instructions about drinking water or bladder preparation.
  • ✓ Bring your referral and relevant previous reports if requested.
  • ✓ Tell the team about your symptoms and any concerns.

Ultrasound does not use ionizing radiation, but not every condition or body structure can be assessed effectively with ultrasound. Follow your clinician's advice.

Questions About Ultrasound?

Does ultrasound use radiation?

No. Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves rather than ionizing radiation.

Is an ultrasound scan painful?

Most ultrasound examinations are generally comfortable. You may feel some pressure from the transducer, especially when the area being examined is tender.

Do I need to fast before my scan?

Some abdominal ultrasound examinations require fasting, while others do not. Follow the instructions provided for your specific appointment.

How long does an ultrasound take?

The duration depends on the body area and type of examination. Your imaging centre can advise you about the expected appointment time.

When will I receive my results?

Reporting times vary according to the examination and clinical workflow. Ask your imaging centre when your report is expected.
